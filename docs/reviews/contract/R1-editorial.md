C. Decisions, with my proposals for Markim
These four are proposals.

1. Contradiction check location. The retriever has no language model, and contradiction is a semantic judgment, so it cannot live there. Proposal: a separate judge, receiving the `shared_structure` and a span, returning a closed verdict (`compativel`, `silencioso`, `contradiz`) with a reason. The judge can be JEV or an LLM, calibrated on the v9 controls (one known convergence, one known absence). I consume the verdict and record its origin. My role is to check that the verdict is coherent with the span, not to replace it.
2. Neighbour requests and caps. Proposal: they count toward a separate neighbour budget per block, logged, distinct from round counts. Without a limit, context reads are unbounded. The numbers are Markim's.
3. Ordering of units from two books. Proposal: group by side, each list ordered by similarity, with a combined rank kept only in the trace. Interleaving by similarity hides which side is thin, and that is exactly what I need to see to attest silence or convergence.
4. Default cut-off and depth. Proposal: no value is chosen before calibration. Values are calibrated on the controls: the known-convergence case must pass and the known-absence case must not. Chosen values enter `cut_off_identity`. If the configuration changes, silence statuses already emitted become invalid for comparison. This is what makes silence verifiable.
